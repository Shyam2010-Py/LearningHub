How to Use LearningHub

A simple, repeatable loop — designed for ECE students.

01 · Study

Read the concept

Work through notes and reference material in the relevant module before touching code or circuits.

02 · Simulate

Test in LogicLab

Use the simulators (gates, adders, flip-flops) to verify the idea works before you build anything.

03 · Practice

Solve and revise

Take quizzes, redo conversion problems and revise until the answer is automatic.

04 · Build

Apply in hardware

Use ESP32 and sensors to turn the concept into a working mini-project.
